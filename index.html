Step 1 of 5

🧰

Prepare your workspace

Work on a clean, stable surface with enough room to handle each component. Keep small parts together and follow the on-screen instruction for the current step.

Take your time. Correct order and safe handling matter more than speed.
🛡️

Equip the wrist strap

Before touching components in either mode, use the Equip Wrist Strap button in the Tools panel. It helps reduce electrostatic-discharge risk when handling hardware.

The game blocks component actions until the wrist strap is equipped.
🧩

Assemble in order

Choose the component shown in the instruction, then drag it onto its highlighted motherboard slot. Complete each step before moving on; the board updates as parts are installed.

Align connectors carefully. Never force a component into a socket or slot.

Use the screwdriver for screws

Equip the screwdriver before installing or removing screws. This also applies to the small retaining screw for the M.2 SSD.

The screwdriver is only needed for screw-related steps; the wrist strap is required for every component.
🔄

Disassemble in reverse

Disassembly starts with the last parts installed. Follow the highlighted motherboard location and select the component the instructions ask you to remove.

Equip the wrist strap before handling parts, and the screwdriver before removing screws.

WHAT IT DOES
Connects the major computer components.
WHERE IT GOES
Inside the case; other components connect to it.

ASSEMBLY TIP Handle the board by its edges and align it with the case standoffs.
